Comment ça marche ?

(how does it work?)

That's the question I ask about most things. I'm Abdelrahman, oPinqzz online, and I've spent most of my life near computers and the internet, mostly trying to find out what is going on inside them.

There's a button in the corner. Press it, or click any paragraph, and you'll see what these words are made of underneath.

Where it started

Minecraft. It was a game, and then at some point I stopped only playing it and started wondering how it worked.

That wondering never went away. It just moved lower: from the game, to the server, to the network, to what the connection is actually saying. These days I like backends, networking and infrastructure, the unglamorous machinery everything else stands on.
